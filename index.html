About InAmigos Foundation

Working together for a better tomorrow

InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 non-profit organization founded on 23 September 2020 by Govind Shukla. Based in Chhattisgarh, the foundation works to create positive social change through community-driven initiatives.

Its initiatives focus on children's education, women's empowerment, animal welfare, environmental protection, community support and youth skill development.
